# Try Lab Onboarding

Open [Lab Onboarding](https://d2m3yolcjdy6d2.cloudfront.net) in your browser.
The interface is in English. No software installation is needed for the teaching activities.

1. Choose **Opentrons Flex** or **Opentrons OT-2** and play the introduction.
   Select a chapter to revisit deck placement, tip pickup or liquid transfer.
2. Choose **Transfer**, **Distribute** or **Serial dilution**, then **Use this workflow**.
   You can also **Start with a blank deck**.
3. Sign in, or create an account with your `@utoronto.ca` email and complete email
   verification. Complete your learner profile when prompted.
4. In **Workflow studio**, arrange the deck, combine steps and change the volumes.
   Save your design, run the teaching model and inspect the result. Change one
   parameter and try again.
5. Open **My experiments** to revisit saved work. Refresh the browser to check that
   you can resume it.

## Explore the rest

| Entry | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| **Meet the instruments** | Both introductions, starter workflows and a blank deck |
| **Learning lab** | First-transfer lesson, dilution workspace and **Can this workflow run?** |
| **Overview / Onboard / Twin** | Register a teaching workcell and prepare its digital representation |
| **Simulation lab** | Inspect simulation options and their availability |
| **Discovery studio / Workflow marketplace** | Explore workflow ideas and templates |
| **Connector explorer** | Learn connector concepts and development mappings |
| **Sim–Real → Connect an instrument** | Inspect Mac setup and instrument connection requirements |
| **Runs / My experiments** | Review workcell runs and saved teaching experiments |

Teaching results are synthetic. They do not establish physical accuracy, collision
safety or validated lab protocols. Real Matterix DT requires a qualified GPU
worker; physical execution additionally requires an approved connector and
instrument commissioning. Those actions remain unavailable until their checks pass.

For feedback, include the page name, steps you took, expected result and observed
result. Screenshots are useful; do not include passwords, verification codes or
pairing credentials.
